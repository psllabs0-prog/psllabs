# Brand voice

## From historical brand reference (`brand.md`)

Status: **draft** — not fully reconciled with live peptide RUO catalog copy.

Documented voice rules in `brand.md`:

- Precise, not hyped.
- Confident, not defensive.
- Educational; treat the reader as capable.
- Reference-grade: cite studies, show COAs where applicable.
- Warm at the edges in founder/FAQ/About; more clinical in product copy.
- Never sound apologetic, hype-driven, or like a generic wellness brand.
- Never: neon/gradient/gym-bro imagery cues listed in `brand.md`.
